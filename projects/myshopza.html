Myshopza: a logo for an e-commerce software product

Outcome A logo for Myshopza's e-commerce software: a shopping bag that also reads as an M, delivered with light and dark lockups, app icons and favicons.

LogoBrand identityUX/UI design
Project overview

Myshopza is e-commerce software that helps businesses sell online. The identity is built around one simple idea: a shopping bag that is also the letter M. Friendly, clear and easy to remember, for the owners who run their store on it every day.

Logo designBrand identityVisual directionApp iconsUX/UI design
RoleLogo, identity and UX/UI
Approach

01 —Problem

Small businesses compare many e-commerce tools that look and sound alike. Myshopza needed a mark people remember after one look, that still feels warm and approachable.

02 —Strategy

Join the two things the brand is about: shopping, as a bag, and the name, as an M. Keep every shape simple enough to read at 16 pixels, and give each half of the bag its own colour family.

03 —Outcome

A shopping bag that reads as an M, a seven-colour palette, three lockups in light and dark, and a full icon set for iOS, Android and the web.

Construction

The handle is one arc centred over the bag. Every corner of the M sits on an eight-part grid.
