Myshopza: a logo for a smart e-commerce platform

Outcome Designed the Myshopza logo: a shopping bag that is also an M, ready for web and mobile with app icons, a splash screen and dark versions.

01
The brief

A shop you can carry, shaped like an M.

Myshopza is smart e-commerce for every business. The logo needed to say "shopping" at a glance and stay friendly at app-icon size.

The answer is a shopping bag that is also the letter M: the handle sits on top, and two folded halves meet in the middle.

The symbol

Read it two ways.

Bag handleThe arc on top turns the M into a shopping bag.

Two foldsBlue on the left, yellow on the right, crossing like a bag's sides.

Letter MThe inner panels meet in a V, the middle of the M.

Four coloursOne family of colours for many kinds of business.

05
Construction

Built on one centre line.

  • The handle is a single arc, centred over the bag.
  • Both folds start at the top corners and cross on the centre line.
  • The two halves mirror each other, so the M stands straight at any size.
