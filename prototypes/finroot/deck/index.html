01 — OverviewOwn product

Money questions,
answered by the plan.

The problem

A household's money is spread over bank apps, bills, policies and people. A simple "can we afford it?" needs a spreadsheet — the one nobody else updates — and the month ends in surprise.

The idea

Split each month across seven buckets on payday and match spending to them. Every rupee already has a place, so the answer is simply what's left in the right bucket.

What I learned

Calm beats clever. A screen works best when it answers one question and shows fewer numbers, larger. Empty states matter as much as full ones — a new user sees them first.

Next

Validate the personas with real interviews, test the payday split with irregular earners, and bring regional languages to farming households.
