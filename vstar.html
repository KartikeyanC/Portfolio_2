Project Overview

VSTAR is a logo and website for a Singapore door and shutter service company, designed to turn a broken door into a service request in two short steps.

Pain

When a door or shutter jams, the business stops with it. The person on the page is standing in front of it, phone in hand, unsure who to call and what it will cost.

Solution

One page answers those worries first, then asks for the job: pick the service, add four details, and WhatsApp opens with the message already written.
